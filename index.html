About

I work where AI meets high-stakes decisions: who qualifies, what gets flagged, and what a team may and may not do with a tool. My background is in regulated housing and real estate, where every decision needed evidence, a clear owner, and a record that could withstand an audit. That discipline carries into AI governance, in any setting where automated systems affect people.

My approach is to find where the real exposure sits, write rules a small team can follow, and connect every document to the others so the whole system holds together.
